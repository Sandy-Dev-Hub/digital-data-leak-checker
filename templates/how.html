How LeakChecker Works

A cybersecurity awareness tool that analyzes patterns in your personal data and provides actionable security recommendations.

1

User Input

The process begins when you enter an email address and phone number. Before analysis, the application validates the information to ensure correct formatting.

  • Email format is checked using regular expressions
  • Phone number must contain exactly 10 digits
2

Risk Analysis

After validation, the system analyzes patterns in the email and phone number to identify potential security risks.

  • Common public email domains may increase exposure risk
  • Short usernames are easier to guess
  • Numbers inside usernames may create predictable patterns
  • Phone numbers with common prefixes are analyzed
  • Sequential digits increase vulnerability
3

Risk Score Calculation

Each detected pattern contributes to a risk score. The system calculates separate scores for email and phone, then combines them.

  • Email Risk Score
  • Phone Risk Score
  • Total Risk Score
4

Risk Classification

Based on the total score, the system categorizes the risk level into three groups:

  • Low Risk — Minimal exposure
  • Medium Risk — Moderate risk
  • High Risk — Higher probability of data exposure
5

Breach Detection

LeakChecker compares your email against a simulated breach dataset representing known cybersecurity incidents. If a match is found, the system displays the platform name, year of the breach, and the type of data exposed.

6

Security Recommendations

After completing the analysis, the system provides tailored security suggestions to improve your online safety.

  • Enable Two Factor Authentication
  • Avoid sharing phone numbers publicly
  • Use strong and unique passwords
  • Monitor account activity regularly

📋 Conclusion

LeakChecker demonstrates how simple pattern analysis and breach monitoring can help you become more aware of your digital security. By analyzing personal data patterns and providing recommendations, the system encourages safer online practices.
