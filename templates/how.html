How LeakChecker Works

A conversation about how we protect and analyze your data.

U

Can you explain what happens when I enter my email address and phone number on the home page?

The process begins with User Input Validation. Before any deep analysis, the application checks your information to ensure it's formatted correctly:

  • The email format is verified using regular expressions to ensure it represents a valid address.
  • The phone number is checked to ensure it contains exactly 10 digits (in standard formats).

Once validated, we securely temporarily store this input in memory for the duration of the analysis.

U

Once my input is validated, how do you determine if my data is risky?

🤖

We perform Pattern Analysis on both your email and phone number to identify potential vulnerabilities. Here’s what we look for:

  • Email Domains: Common public domains might increase exposure risk compared to private ones.
  • Username Structure: Short usernames or usernames containing raw numbers can create predictable patterns that attackers exploit.
  • Phone Numbers: We analyze prefixes and look for sequential digits that might make the number easier to guess or spoof.

Each detected pattern contributes to an overall Risk Score.

U

Can you break down how the Risk Score is calculated?

🤖

Absolutely. The system calculates separate scores which are then combined:

We calculate an Email Risk Score and a Phone Risk Score independently. Based on the total accumulated score, we categorize your risk level into three groups:

  • Low Risk: Minimal exposure. Your data structure is standard and doesn't exhibit highly predictable patterns.
  • Medium Risk: Moderate risk. There might be some predictable elements in your username or number.
  • High Risk: Higher probability of data exposure due to multiple vulnerabilities detected.

This score doesn't mean you've been hacked, just that the structure of your data is statistically more vulnerable to targeted scraping or dictionary attacks.

U

What about the Breach Detection part? How do you check if I've actually been compromised?

🤖

For Breach Detection, LeakChecker compares your email against a dataset representing known cybersecurity incidents. If a match is found in our system, we immediately alert you.

We display the platform name where the breach occurred, the year of the incident, and most importantly, the type of data that was exposed (like passwords, IP addresses, or personal messages).

What should I do if my Risk Score is high or you find a breach?

🤖

After completing the analysis, we don't just leave you with bad news. We provide tailored Security Recommendations to improve your posture.

Common recommendations include:

  • Enabling Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on your most important accounts.
  • Changing compromised passwords immediately to strong, unique alternatives.
  • Removing your phone number from public social media profiles if possible.
  • Using our Password Checker tool to verify your password strength.

The goal of LeakChecker isn't just to report issues, but to help you actively become more secure and aware of your digital footprint.
